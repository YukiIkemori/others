1. LEAN: moderate and elegant, about 12 degrees, no more. In the profile rows (3, 4) the centre of the head is only about 8 art pixels ahead of the hips; the torso stays long and clearly visible, he is not crouched or hunched. The runner stays TALL: in the contact frames (columns 2 and 4) the top of his hair is only 3-4 art pixels lower than the top of the standing figure's hair in column 1; in the flight frames (columns 3 and 5) it is level with it.
2. FLIGHT FRAMES: the legs are not tucked up tight under the body: one leg reaches forward and down, the other stretches out behind; the lowest boot is only about 3 art pixels above the ground line.
3. CAPE SIZE: the cape keeps its real size: it is the same short hip-length cape as on the standing figure. Its streaming part is never longer than the standing cape is tall, and never wider than one and a half head widths beyond the body.
4. ROW 1 (front view): the cape flies BEHIND him, away from the viewer. We see it as cloth flaring out behind both shoulders and upper arms, about equally on the viewer's left and right, in ALL four frames; only the hem ripples a little differently from frame to frame. It never becomes a long wing out to one side only.
5. ROW 2 (back view): the cape hangs over the back and is lifted toward the viewer by the wind, its hem raised to waist height and flaring a little to both sides, about equally, in ALL four frames. It never becomes a long wing out to one side only.
6. The lantern stays close to the left hip, tilted back on its short chain (it does not fly far from the body).
